WalkAway Privacy Policy

Publisher: Shane
Contact: shanewalkaway@gmail.com
Effective date: 13 October 2026
App: WalkAway for Android, distributed through Google Play

1. The short version

  • WalkAway works on your phone. Your settings, walk records and bank minutes stay on your phone.
  • We do not run analytics, show ads, or ask you for an account. We do not sell data.
  • Precise location is used only during a walk you start. It is not saved and not uploaded.
  • Google Play handles payments. Once a week, the app downloads a public list of apps that can never be blocked.

2. What WalkAway accesses

Accessibility Service

WalkAway uses Android's Accessibility Service to see which app is open in front of you, so it can block the apps you chose.

  • It reads only the app's package name, a technical ID such as com.example.app, plus the window type, so it can ignore keyboards and pop-ups. It notices every app switch, not only the apps you block. It keeps only the current app in memory, and saves nothing except a daily count of times you opened a blocked app. It does not read screen content, messages, passwords, web pages or anything you type, and nothing it sees leaves your phone.
  • You can turn it off at any time in Android Settings, Accessibility. Blocking then stops.

Location

  • WalkAway asks for location permission. Android lets you choose precise or approximate; walks need precise, so if you choose approximate a walk will not start and the app explains why. Location is used only during a walk you start, with a notification showing for the whole walk. Each reading is used in memory to measure how far you are from where you started, then thrown away. The starting point is also kept in memory only. WalkAway does not ask for background location. Location readings come from Google Play services on your phone; Google's handling of location is governed by your device's location settings and Google's privacy policy.
  • We do not store coordinates or your route. The starting point is held in memory only and is not saved.
  • Your location is not uploaded, sold or shared.
  • You can stop a walk at any time, or turn off location in Android Settings.

Apps on your phone

To show the list you pick from, WalkAway reads the list of apps on your phone that can be opened. This stays on your phone. Only the apps you choose to block are saved.

Notifications

  • WalkAway uses notifications for the walk-in-progress message, the "Blocking is off" alert and, if you turn them on, a daily walk reminder (at most one a day).
  • You can allow or deny notifications. WalkAway still works if you deny them, but you may miss the "Blocking is off" alert.

3. What is stored on your phone

This data is stored only on your phone:

  • The apps you chose to block, and your settings (distance, unlock length, and schedules if you have Pro).
  • The built-in and downloaded lists of apps that can't be blocked.
  • Technical time records the app uses to keep unlock timing accurate.
  • Walk records: when a walk started and ended, whether it counted, how far it reached, and how many readings were skipped. They also hold the target distance and reward type. No coordinates are included.
  • Bank: minutes you earned by walking, when they expire, and a log of when you used them.
  • Weekly stats: number of walks, distance, and the number of times you opened a blocked app. They also hold the minutes of bank time used per day.
  • Your Pro subscription status, saved from Google Play: which plan you have and when it was last checked, so Pro keeps working for up to 7 days without a connection.
  • Simple screen flags: which setup steps and disclosures you have accepted, and when you last saw the Pro offer.

WalkAway is designed so its data is not included in Android backups or phone-to-phone transfers, so restoring a phone or moving to a new phone starts WalkAway fresh.

4. What leaves your phone

  • Google Play Billing. Google Play handles trials, subscriptions and payments under Google's privacy policy. WalkAway does not run a server and never sees your card details. Google Play tells the app whether you have Pro, and the app keeps that on your phone. Google gives us order information in the Google Play Console (such as order number, date, product, price and country, and possibly other details Google provides). We use it only for billing, refunds and support.
  • Safelist download. About once a week, WalkAway downloads a public list of apps that can never be blocked (for example phone, messages, settings, maps, authenticator and banking apps). The request contains no account and no device ID. The web host that serves the file can see your IP address, the time and app or browser details (user agent) as with any web request. We do not receive or keep those request logs. If the download fails, WalkAway keeps using the list built into the app.
  • Google Play Console. Google gives us summary figures (installs, uninstalls, subscriptions) and crash/"app not responding" reports. Android collects these from people who allowed usage and diagnostics sharing in their device settings. Crash reports can include phone model, Android version and technical error details. WalkAway itself contains no crash-reporting or analytics software.

WalkAway itself sends nothing else. Information only reaches us if you choose to send it, for example by emailing us (see "Information you send us").

Information you send us

  • Email. If you email shanewalkaway@gmail.com or use "Report a missing app" (which opens your email app), we receive your email address and what you write. We use it only to reply and to improve the list of apps that can't be blocked. We keep it only as long as needed, and delete it sooner on request.
  • Beta diagnostics (test versions only). "Share diagnostics" shows a preview, and nothing is sent unless you share it. It includes totals only: walks started and completed, why location readings were skipped, bank-minute use, phone model and Android version. It has no location, no app names and no identifiers. It is deleted when the test ends.
  • Testers. We hold the email address you gave us to add you to the Google Play test, and we remove it when the test ends.

5. What we do not collect

  • No analytics software, and no server that receives your app data.
  • No ads.
  • WalkAway has no account and does not ask for your name, email or phone number. (Google Play uses your Google account for purchases.)
  • No coordinates, routes or maps.
  • We do not sell your data, and we do not share it for advertising.

6. Children

WalkAway is not directed to children under 13. It is intended for people aged 16 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, or from anyone under 16 in the UK or EEA. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, email shanewalkaway@gmail.com and we will delete it.

7. How long data is kept, and how to delete it

  • Information you send us is kept only as long as needed (see "Information you send us") and deleted sooner on request.
  • Everything WalkAway stores is kept on your phone until you delete it, clear WalkAway's storage or uninstall the app. Exceptions: Banked minutes expire 7 days after they are earned (minutes earned by walking further expire 7 days after that walk ends). Expired minutes can no longer be used. Walk records, the log of when you used bank minutes, and weekly stats are kept until you clear storage or uninstall. The free version shows only the current week, but older records stay on your phone. To delete everything: Android Settings > Apps > WalkAway > Storage > Clear storage, or uninstall.
  • We keep no copy of the data stored on your phone, so only you can delete it. To delete emails or diagnostics you sent us, email shanewalkaway@gmail.com.
  • Google keeps purchase and subscription records under its own policies. To cancel, use Google Play, Subscriptions.

8. Changes to this policy

We will post any update on this page with a new effective date. For significant changes we will also say so in the app's release notes before they take effect.

9. Your rights by country

You can ask us what information we hold about you (normally only emails you sent us) and ask us to delete it. Data on your phone is under your control.

WalkAway is available in the US, UK, Canada, Australia, Ireland and New Zealand.
